FiveM · Client

Client-side asset mods for multiplayer servers. FiveM installs wherever you put it — typically under your own AppData\Local rather than a shared location — so there is no reliable path to detect and this one is always set by hand.

FiveM · Converter

Converts a single-player add-on vehicle pack straight into a FiveM resource, reading dlc.rpf directly — including the nested vehicles.rpf inside it — with no OpenIV or CodeWalker extraction step. Vehicle and add-on packs only; script mod conversion is not built.

Only convert content you have the right to redistribute. Converting a pack does not grant you permission to share it.
