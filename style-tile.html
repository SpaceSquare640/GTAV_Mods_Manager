GTAV Mods Manager — Style Tile

Proposed visual language. Dark-first Slate base with a full light counterpart, brand orange sampled from the app icon, and four re-picked mode identities. Nothing here is a finished screen — this page exists so the direction can be approved before the full mockup is built.

Surfaces & text

Slate, from the ui-ux-pro-max “Developer Tool / IDE” palette. Contrast ratios below are computed live against the current theme’s page background.

Brand

Sampled from GTAV_Mods_Manager_App_Icon.png — the icon’s warm orange sits at hue 18–36° over a dark blue-black field that already matches Slate. Orange is reserved for brand and primary actions only; it is no longer any mode’s identity colour.

Mode identities

All four hues re-picked. Legacy and Enhanced are the pair most easily confused — same game, different edition — so they sit at opposite ends of the usable band. None of them borrows a hue already spoken for by brand orange, success green, warning yellow or danger red, so a mode colour can never read as a status. LSPDFR keeps its gradient treatment rather than becoming a fourth flat hue, because it is a sub-tab under both editions.

Components

Density is medium: 40px table rows and 12–16px gutters. Denser than the previous design so more mods fit on screen, but not the 36px/8px maximum-density preset — rows carry Enable, Disable and Uninstall controls that need a comfortable hit area.

The fix for the layout problem in the old design: the selector column on the left stays put while the content column on the right fills the available height and scrolls on its own. Replaces the old fixed max-height: 300px box. Used by the Terms screen and the file editor. Scroll inside the right column to see it work.

1. No warranty

GTAV Mods Manager is provided free of charge, for non-commercial use only. It is offered as-is, with no guarantee against game instability, save corruption, or online-service action resulting from third-party mods you choose to install.

2. Official builds only

Support and safety guarantees apply only to builds downloaded from this project’s official GitHub Releases. Modified or third-party redistributions are used entirely at your own risk.

3. Your responsibility

You are responsible for reviewing what a mod does before installing it. The conflict checker and protected-file guard reduce risk — they do not eliminate it.

4. Protected files

The application refuses to write to game executables, Rockstar online components and anti-cheat binaries. This guard cannot be overridden from the interface, by a profile, or by the AI assistant.
